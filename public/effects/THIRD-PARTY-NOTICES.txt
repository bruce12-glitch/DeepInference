ASCII Text Lab (src/effects/30-ascii-text-lab) lays text out with a port of FIGlet's layout
code (figlet.c) and bundles twelve FIGlet fonts, all from FIGlet 2.2.5
(https://github.com/cmatsuoka/figlet, tag 2.2.5): standard.flf by Glenn Chappell and Ian Chai,
and slant.flf, big.flf, block.flf, shadow.flf, small.flf, smslant.flf, mini.flf, lean.flf,
script.flf, bubble.flf and digital.flf by Glenn Chappell. Some carry additions by John Cowan and
header updates by Paul Burton, as their notice lines say. build/subset-figlet-fonts.mjs keeps
each font's notice lines and its glyphs for ASCII 32-126 and the seven German characters, and
adds a comment line naming the change, as the fonts ask. The Solid and Solid Slant fonts are
not separate files: src/effects/30-ascii-text-lab/solid.mjs draws block.flf's letters filled at
runtime (each `_|` pair a solid block, half a block in the outer corner of a diagonal step, Solid
Slant leaning them one column every two rows) and adds a shadow of box-drawing lines worked out
from their shapes; that derivation is by UX Pilot (uxpilot.ai), 13 Oct 2026, and block.flf itself
is bundled unchanged. FIGlet 2.2.5 is distributed under this licence:

in colour palettes made and shared by pixel artists, used as their authors allow:

(https://github.com/nesbox/TIC-80), distributed there under the MIT License (its text is above)
with the notice "Copyright (c) 2017 Vadim Grigoruk".

"Feel free to use it, and modify".

sprites, games and pixel art.
Resurrect 64 by Kerrie Lake (https://lospec.com/palette-list/resurrect-64), use allowed by the
author.
DawnBringer 16 by Richard "DawnBringer" Fhager
(https://pixeljoint.com/forum/forum_posts.asp?TID=12795), shared as free to use.
